About & Verification Policy

Mission

AidFrontUA is an independent, non-partisan documentation and indexing initiative. Our purpose is twofold: to maintain an accessible, structured directory of legitimate worldwide aid mechanisms supporting Ukraine, and to preserve a factual, chronological log of international reporting on the ongoing war.

Zero Financial Intermediation. We operates strictly as an informational index. We do not collect, process, or hold financial donations, nor do we accept paid placements. All links redirect directly to the verified official portals of sovereign governments, UN agencies, and audited humanitarian organizations.

AidFrontUA stands in unconditional solidarity with Ukraine and its people in the face of the Russian Federation’s unprovoked military aggression. We unequivocally condemn the invasion, war crimes, and systematic state-sponsored disinformation campaigns aimed at distorting reality. Our stance is rooted in international law, human dignity, and love for a sovereign Ukraine—not hostility toward any people, but resolute opposition to aggression, imperialism, and propaganda.

Verification Standards

  • Aid Programs: Every listed initiative undergoes manual verification to confirm official institutional standing, registered NGO status, or direct governmental mandate prior to publication.
  • Domain Integrity: Destination URLs are authenticated against official government registries and established non-profit directories to protect users against fraudulent or lookalike domains.
  • News Dispatches: Entries in the Daily War Log link exclusively to established international news agencies, primary official statements, and recognized journalistic outlets.
  • Submissions & Corrections: To propose a vetted aid program or report a broken link, send us an email to contact@aidfrontua.com.
