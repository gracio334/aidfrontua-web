About & Verification Policy

Mission

AidFrontUA is an independent, non-partisan documentation and indexing initiative. Our purpose is twofold: to maintain an accessible, structured directory of legitimate worldwide aid mechanisms supporting Ukraine, and to preserve a factual, chronological log of international reporting on the ongoing war.

Verification Standards

  • Aid Programs: Every listed initiative undergoes manual verification to confirm official institutional standing, registered NGO status, or direct governmental mandate prior to publication.
  • News Dispatches: Entries in the Daily War Log link exclusively to established international news agencies, primary official statements, and recognized journalistic outlets.
  • Submissions & Corrections: To propose a vetted aid program or report a broken link, contact contact@aidfrontua.com.
