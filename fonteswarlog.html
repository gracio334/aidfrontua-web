Verification Sources & Reference Outlets

1. Official & Institutional Sources (Ukraine)

Essential for official data on shelters, humanitarian corridors, government decrees, and affected-infrastructure statistics.

  • Ukrinform – Ukrainian National News Agency
  • State Emergency Service of Ukraine (DSNS)
  • Ministry for Reintegration of Temporarily Occupied Territories
  • Ministry of Social Policy of Ukraine

2. Independent Ukrainian Reference Media

Independent journalism produced on the ground, with a strong editorial standard and English-language coverage focused on the human and social impact.

  • The Kyiv Independent
  • Euromaidan Press
  • The New Voice of Ukraine (NV)

3. Major International Agencies (Cross-Verification)

To ensure impartiality and cross-check data before publication in the Daily War Log.

  • Reuters
  • Associated Press (AP)
